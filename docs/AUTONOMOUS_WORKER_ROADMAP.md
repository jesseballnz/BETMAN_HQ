# BETMAN-WORKER Opportunity Roadmap

Build capabilities in economic order. Each skill must have trusted inputs, a measurable outcome and a constrained action surface.

## 1. Acquisition optimiser — building now

Joins Meta spend to signups, activated trials and paid customers. Recommends budget allocation and later executes inside hard limits.

**Outcome:** lower paid CAC and faster learning.

## 2. Attribution and experiment controller — next

Issues canonical campaign/ad/creative identifiers, validates UTMs, persists identity through Core and Stripe, assigns experiment cells and stops contaminated tests.

**Outcome:** trustworthy unit economics and defensible growth data.

## 3. Creative laboratory

Extracts winning hooks, creates controlled image/video/copy variants, produces Meta drafts, detects fatigue and preserves a searchable creative-performance library.

**Outcome:** compound creative intelligence competitors cannot copy.

## 4. Conversion surgeon

Measures landing, signup, verification, trial activation, first RacingOS session, checkout and purchase. Finds the highest-value leak and proposes one experiment at a time.

**Outcome:** turn cheap traffic into customers.

## 5. Trial and retention operator

Builds lifecycle cohorts, identifies expiring or inactive trials, drafts compliant win-back/onboarding messages and optimises activation based on product usage.

**Outcome:** higher trial-to-paid conversion and longer retention.

## 6. Race-day demand forecaster

Uses the racing calendar, meeting quality, jackpots, market activity, weather and historic conversion to predict when acquisition and retention activity will be most valuable.

**Outcome:** spend when punter intent is naturally highest.

## 7. Customer intelligence miner

Aggregates support themes, search behaviour, onboarding answers and product usage without exposing customer data. Converts recurring problems into ranked product opportunities.

**Outcome:** faster product-market learning and better retention.

## 8. Commercial prospecting operator

Finds and scores trainers, syndicators, breeders, media partners and professional punters; prepares evidence-led outreach drafts but never sends without authority.

**Outcome:** B2B revenue and distribution.

## 9. Pricing and unit-economics controller

Tracks cohort revenue, retention, refunds, payment failures and contribution margin. Replaces provisional CAC caps with evidence-based acquisition limits.

**Outcome:** profitable scaling rather than subscriber vanity.

## 10. Competitive and market radar

Tracks competitor launches, pricing, racing-media narratives and advertising patterns. Produces concise opportunity alerts tied to BETMAN products.

**Outcome:** earlier moves and a stronger information moat.

## 11. Platform reliability guardian

Observes service health and customer-impacting failures, correlates incidents with acquisition and retention, and opens a recovery playbook. It does not mutate Production without explicit authority.

**Outcome:** protect trust and prevent paid traffic landing on broken systems.

## Priority rule

Do not build the next autonomous action surface until the current one has reliable measurement, explicit authority, idempotency, rollback and an auditable outcome.
